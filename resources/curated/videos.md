# OS Development Video Resources

Categorized list of real YouTube channels, series, and playlists for learning operating-system development, low-level/system programming, and each language covered on this hub (C, C++, Rust, Go, C#). Every link was verified reachable at curation time. For series, prefer the playlist URLs so the videos play in order.

## OS Dev YouTubers

- **Nanobyte** – Building-an-OS series plus short explainers on OS internals; ships a full written companion tutorial at [nanobyte.dev](https://nanobyte.dev). https://www.youtube.com/@nanobyte-dev
- **Andreas Kling (SerenityOS / Ladybird)** – Raw development sessions building the SerenityOS kernel, its Unix-like userland, and the Ladybird browser. https://www.youtube.com/@awesomekling
- **Low Level Learning** – Low-level / systems programming, reverse engineering, and bare-metal explainers oriented at developers going below user space. https://www.youtube.com/@LowLevelTV
- **David Callanan (CodePulse)** – The *OS Dev* series walking through writing a small OS in C/C++ from an empty boot sector up through protected mode and a kernel. https://www.youtube.com/@CodePulse
- **dr Jonas Birch** – Low-level programming made accessible; extensive C and assembly playlists plus a full MS-DOS/OS-in-C project series. https://www.youtube.com/@dr-Jonas-Birch
- **Developed From Scratch** – Live dev streams building an OS, a compiler, and a build system entirely from scratch. https://www.youtube.com/@DevelopedFromScratch
- **Olivier** – The *Creating My Own Operating System* video (and related low-level experiments) done live from scratch. https://www.youtube.com/@O_li_vier
- **NCommander** – OS archaeology and reverse engineering (bootstrapping old toolchains, kernel history); a good primer on why OS dev is the way it is. https://www.youtube.com/@NCommander
- **hhp3 (Harry Porter)** – Professor-level walkthroughs of the MIT xv6 kernel (RISC-V) and RISC-V architecture for kernel programmers. https://www.youtube.com/@hhp3

## Compiler, Debugger & Low-Level Tooling Channels

- **Matt Godbolt** – Compiler Explorer's author; talks on assembly output, compilers turning C into machine code. https://www.youtube.com/@MattGodbolt
- **CppCon** – Conference talks including deep compiler, ABI, memory, and low-level C++ sessions. https://www.youtube.com/@CppCon
- **Tsoding** – Live low-level programming: compilers, interpreters, ray tracers, and systems hacking in C/C++. https://www.youtube.com/@TsodingDaily
- **Low Level Learning** – see above; strong coverage of debugging binaries and reading assembly. https://www.youtube.com/@LowLevelTV

## Computer Architecture & Systems Channels

- **Ben Eater** – Builds an 8-bit computer and a 6502 machine on breadboards, teaching digital logic and assembly from the ground up — essential mental model for OS dev. https://www.youtube.com/@BenEater
- **Core Dumped** – Computer architecture and low-level engineering explainers (pipelining, memory, CPU design). https://www.youtube.com/@CoreDumpped
- **LiveOverflow** – Exploit development and reverse engineering; great for understanding memory, stacks, and CPU behavior. https://www.youtube.com/@LiveOverflow
- **Computerphile** – Accessible explainers on CPUs, memory, kernels, and how computers actually work. https://www.youtube.com/@Computerphile
- **3Blue1Brown** – Not OS-specific, but the best intuition-building math/animation channel for the fundamentals behind computing. https://www.youtube.com/@3blue1brown
- **Dave's Garage** – Dave Plummer (ex-Microsoft); OS, kernel and low-level history plus hands-on demos. https://www.youtube.com/@davesgarage
- **ThePrimeagen** – Systems programming, algorithms, and candid takes on writing low-level code. https://www.youtube.com/@ThePrimeagen
